Full recap: get your export in 3 steps

  1. On a computer, open garmin.com/account/datamanagement and sign in with the account your watch uses. The phone app can't export.
  2. Choose Export Your Data, then Request Data Export. Garmin emails you a download link, usually within 24–48 hours.
  3. Download the ZIP from that email (the link expires) and drop it above. No need to unzip it.

In a hurry? Quick recap from your activities list

  1. In your browser, open connect.garmin.com and go to Activities → All Activities.
  2. Scroll to the very bottom and keep going until no more activities load. Garmin only exports the rows on screen, which is 20 until you scroll.
  3. Click Export CSV at the top right and drop the file above. You'll get the activity cards right away; sleep, stress and heart cards need the full export.
